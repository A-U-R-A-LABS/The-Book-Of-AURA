An AURA Engineer is someone who:

Understands deeply.

Designs carefully.

Builds responsibly.

Explains clearly.

Learns continuously.

Improves relentlessly.

# Engineering Decision Framework

Every engineering decision inside AURA follows the same process.

---

## Step 1

Understand

What problem are we solving?

---

## Step 2

Research

Has this problem been solved before?

What can we learn?

---

## Step 3

Design

Can we design something simpler?

---

## Step 4

Evaluate

Advantages

Disadvantages

Risks

Long-term impact

---

## Step 5

Decide

Choose one solution.

Explain why.

---

## Step 6

Document

Every important decision is recorded.

---

## Step 7

Review

Was it the right decision?

What did we learn?
