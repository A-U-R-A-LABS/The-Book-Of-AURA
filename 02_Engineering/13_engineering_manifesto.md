# AURA Engineering Manifesto

## We believe engineering is thinking.

Engineering starts with understanding.

Programming starts with implementation.

We never confuse the two.

---

## We design before we build.

Architecture is not documentation.

Architecture is a decision.

Every line of code should have a place in the architecture.

---

## We solve problems.

Technology is not the goal.

Technology is a tool.

The problem always comes first.

---

## We value clarity over complexity.

Simple systems survive.

Complex systems collapse.

We choose simplicity whenever possible.

---

## We explain every important decision.

Trust is built through transparency.

Every recommendation.

Every architecture.

Every engineering decision.

Should be explainable.

---

## We document our knowledge.

Knowledge locked inside one engineer
is knowledge that will eventually disappear.

Documentation preserves engineering.

---

## We improve continuously.

Every release teaches us.

Every mistake improves us.

Every project makes us stronger.

Continuous improvement is part of engineering.

---

## We build for decades.

We don't optimize only for today.

We build systems that future engineers
will enjoy maintaining.

---

## We respect evidence.

Opinions start discussions.

Evidence ends discussions.

Whenever possible,

data wins.

---

## We build responsibly.

Our systems affect people.

Therefore every decision carries responsibility.

Engineering without ethics
is incomplete engineering.
